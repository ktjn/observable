## Approval prerequisites

- **Ledger and reconciliation.** FinOps selects an approved ledger extract, its owner, currencies and period boundaries, classification rules, correction policy and reconciliation tolerance. Identify who may import, who independently approves the period total and who approves the resulting report. Bind each line to a tenant, source record and revision. Keep existing expenditure records outside the classified report until a traceable classification exists.
- **Estimation and value.** Select the planning horizon, approved rates and volume inputs for coverage and retention estimates. Name the sources for asset cost, operational use, criticality, risk and expected benefit, and who may review each service.
- **Policy enforcement.** Identify the provider and authoritative apply operation for each coverage, sampling or retention change, the required diagnostic, SLO and audit evidence, and the authority that can grant a scoped exception. Define scope, expiry, policy revision and affected evidence before implementation.
